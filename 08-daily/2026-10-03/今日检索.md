- **英文摘要**：We propose ABrA-GD (Adaptive Bregman Accelerated Gradient Descent), an adaptive algorithm for relatively smooth convex optimization. ### 3. Is Weight Tying Still Beneficial for Decoder-Only LLMs in Private Settings Under DP-SGD? — 研究优先级 8.29

- **来源**：arxiv
- **主题（大方向）**：随机梯度与优化理论
- **细分领域**：随机梯度与收敛性
- **题目中文翻译**：在 DP-SGD 私有训练中，权重共享对仅解码器大语言模型仍然有益吗？
- **摘要授权与署名**：Razan El Mais, Ali Chehab, Ibrahim Issa, Razane Tajeddine；[原始论文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40335v1)；[CC BY 4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)。中文摘要为完整翻译。
- **作者**：Razan El Mais, Ali Chehab, Ibrahim Issa, Razane Tajeddine
- **英文摘要**：Differentially Private Stochastic Gradient Descent (DP-SGD) is a leading approach for privacy-preserving fine-tuning of large language models (LLMs). Many decoder-only LLMs employ weight tying between input and output embeddings, a design choice originally introduced for parameter efficiency and improved language modeling performance in the non-private setting. However, the impact of weight tying under differentially private training remains largely unexplored. In this work, we investigate the role of weight tying in the DP setting using GPT2 and DistilGPT2 as representative decoder-only architectures. Interestingly, we find that untied embeddings consistently outperform weight-tied models under DP-SGD, achieving gains of up to 4.74% points in accuracy on SST-2, QNLI, and QQP. Beyond improved utility, untying embeddings enables the use of memory-efficient ghost clipping for DP-SGD. By contrast, weight tying introduces shared-parameter interactions that complicate standard ghost norm computation and largely negate its computational advantages. As a result, untied models achieve over 60% lower memory usage while preserving the benefits of ghost clipping. Our results indicate that untied embeddings provide a more effective and scalable design for differentially private training of decoder-only LLMs and highlight the need to revisit standard LLM architectural choices in the privacy-preserving setting.
- **中文摘要（完整翻译）**：差分隐私随机梯度下降（DP-SGD）是对大语言模型（LLM）进行隐私保护微调的主要方法之一。许多仅解码器 LLM 在输入与输出嵌入之间采用权重共享；这一设计最初是为了在非隐私场景中提高参数效率和语言建模性能。然而，权重共享对差分隐私训练的影响仍在很大程度上尚未得到探索。本文以 GPT2 和 DistilGPT2 作为代表性的仅解码器架构，研究权重共享在差分隐私（DP）场景中的作用。我们发现，在 DP-SGD 下，不共享权重的嵌入模型持续优于权重共享模型，在 SST-2、QNLI 和 QQP 上的准确率提升最高达到 4.74 个百分点。除提高效用之外，解除嵌入权重共享还使 DP-SGD 能够使用节省显存的 ghost clipping。相比之下，权重共享引入了共享参数之间的交互，使标准 ghost norm 的计算复杂化，并在很大程度上抵消了其计算优势。因此，不共享权重的模型在保留 ghost clipping 优势的同时，显存使用量降低超过 60%。我们的结果表明，不共享权重的嵌入为仅解码器 LLM 的差分隐私训练提供了更有效且更易扩展的设计，并凸显了重新审视隐私保护场景中标准 LLM 架构选择的必要性。
- **一句话总结**：作者报告解除输入/输出 embedding 共享在小型 decoder 模型 DP-SGD 微调中提高准确率，并恢复 ghost clipping 的显存收益。
- **摘要声称的核心贡献**：
  - 摘要给出架构选择与 per-example norm/ghost clipping 实现耦合的可核验机制。
  - 报告任务准确率与显存收益，提供小预算复核入口。
