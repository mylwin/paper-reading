\begin{abstract}
This manuscript is a synthetic skill-evaluation draft, not a report of completed research.
We specify Confidence Gate, a rule applied to the predictions of a fixed classifier:
a prediction is accepted when its maximum class probability is at least $0.8$ and
rejected otherwise. The rule is defined without modifying the classifier.
The supplied material contains no dataset, named model, experimental measurements,
implementation, related literature, or theoretical result. Accordingly, this draft
formalizes the decision rule and identifies the information needed for evaluation,
without claiming improved performance, novelty, or a correctness guarantee.
The threshold is a supplied design choice whose empirical justification remains
unavailable. All research conclusions beyond the stated rule require additional
author evidence and review.
\end{abstract}
