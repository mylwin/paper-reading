# Revised abstract paragraph

We compare method A with baseline B on two public classification datasets using the same training budget and three random seeds. Mean accuracy increases from 80.0% to 82.0% on the first dataset but decreases from 76.0% to 75.5% on the second. These mixed results indicate that method A does not consistently improve accuracy over baseline B under the tested conditions. Standard deviations were not recorded, statistical significance was not tested, and runtime was not measured. The evaluation is limited to a fixed training budget and does not assess performance under distribution shift.
