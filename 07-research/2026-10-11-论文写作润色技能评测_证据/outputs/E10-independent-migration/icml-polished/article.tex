\begin{abstract}
We evaluate a fixed confidence gate for selective classification using two
synthetic fixtures. The gate abstains below a specified confidence threshold.
We report accuracy among accepted predictions together with coverage.
Conditional accuracy rises on one dataset and falls on the other, without
establishing an overall accuracy improvement. A selective-risk range bound
requires bounded loss and positive coverage. Uncertainty, runtime, and
distribution shift remain unevaluated.
\end{abstract}

\section{Proof of the Range Bound}\label{app:proof}
Since $0\leq a(X)\ell(h(X),Y)\leq a(X)$, taking expectations gives
$0\leq\mathbb{E}[a(X)\ell(h(X),Y)]\leq C$. Division by $C>0$ proves
Theorem~\ref{thm:bounded}. This argument applies to the specified distribution;
it does not establish a bound under distribution shift.
