009                                               A BSTRACT
010
011            This manuscript is a synthetic skill-evaluation draft, not a report of completed re-
012            search. We specify Confidence Gate, a rule applied to the predictions of a fixed
013            classifier: a prediction is accepted when its maximum class probability is at least
014            0.8 and rejected otherwise. The rule is defined without modifying the classifier.
015            The supplied material contains no dataset, named model, experimental measure-
016            ments, implementation, related literature, or theoretical result. Accordingly, this
017            draft formalizes the decision rule and identifies the information needed for evalua-
018
               tion, without claiming improved performance, novelty, or a correctness guarantee.
               The threshold is a supplied design choice whose empirical justification remains
019
               unavailable. All research conclusions beyond the stated rule require additional
020
               author evidence and review.

      1   S COPE AND P ROBLEM
024
025
      A classifier prediction may be passed through an acceptance rule that determines whether the pre-
026   diction is retained or rejected. Confidence Gate uses only the largest class probability reported for
027   that prediction. The supplied proposal sets the threshold to 0.8 and keeps the classifier fixed. This
028   manuscript specifies that behavior; it does not establish whether the rule is useful on any particular
029   task.
