056
057   Since 0 ≤ a(X)ℓ(h(X), Y ) ≤ a(X), taking expectations
058   gives 0 ≤ E[a(X)ℓ(h(X), Y )] ≤ C. Division by C > 0
059   proves Theorem 1. This argument applies to the specified
060   distribution; it does not establish a bound under distribution
061   shift.
